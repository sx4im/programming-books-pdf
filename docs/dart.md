## For Beginners

These books introduce Dart's syntax and the basics of building cross-platform apps, often through the Flutter framework.

- **Dart Apprentice: Fundamentals** (*1st Edition*)
- **Flutter Apprentice** (*4th Edition*)
- **Dart: Up and Running** (*1st Edition*)

## For Intermediate Developers

Move beyond the basics to build robust, well-structured applications with Dart and Flutter.

- **Programming Flutter** (*2020 Edition*)
- **Flutter in Action** (*1st Edition*)
- **Dart Apprentice: Beyond the Basics** (*1st Edition*)
- **Pragmatic Flutter** (*1st Edition*)
